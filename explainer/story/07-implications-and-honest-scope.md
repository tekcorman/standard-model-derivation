# Chapter 7 — Implications

The structural chain is complete. This chapter steps back and names what it implies for physics.

## Plurality is structural, not anomaly

The retention threshold ([Chapter 4](04-recurrence-and-the-mdl-waterline.md)) keeps every compression that pays for itself. Chirality is both-handed. Generations are all three. Walk windings are all retained. The framework's "uniqueness" claims are **dominance** claims within the dominant retained sector. Subdominant retentions exist by structure; the Cabibbo–Kobayashi–Maskawa element $V_{cb}$ as a geometric series over winding numbers is one example, the framework's neutrino mixing structure is another.

## Three commitments + one empirical labeling are the irreducible minimum

Self-containment + finite observer + active reading, plus an empirical labeling rule that identifies which substrate eigenvalues correspond to which observed masses. Every other structural ingredient — the binary toggle, the free involutive monoid, the description-length retention threshold, complex Hilbert space, local fermionic statistics, the operator catalog of physics — is theorem.

## The fundamental operators of physics are recurrence mechanisms

Hamiltonians generate time-evolution recurrence. Clifford generators generate the local fermionic algebra at trivalent nodes. Gauge transformations generate the redundancies under which recurrence is invariant. **They are not imposed from outside. They are catalogued by what survives.**

## Field selection is structural, not stipulated

Complex over real is forced by the requirement that spectral data fit in a finite real-valued register. A real Hilbert space has imaginary spectrum (unstorable in a real register); a complex Hilbert space has real spectrum (storable). Quaternionic Hilbert space is similarly excluded. No external choice of field is made; the empirical labeling rule is not invoked. This is **sharper than the standard operational derivations** of complex quantum mechanics (Hardy 2001; Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti 2011; Masanes–Mueller 2011), which presuppose an operational scope.

## Causal structure is free

Once toggles are in place, before/after is automatic. The substrate's Cayley graph is a causal graph in the same minimal sense any "A affects B" structure is. The framework's leading-order Lorentz invariance is the *discovery* of Lorentz invariance, not its imposition; the residual anisotropy at the substrate scale is sub-luminal.

## The continuum is sub-Planckian

Rapid decay of toggle correlations on the substrate (correlation length about $0.6\,\ell_P$) is what licenses Stone's theorem for the continuum-time limit. Without it, the unitary continuum limit does not exist.

## The Standard Model is what falls out

The structural chain produces the *class* of theories that compress recurrence onto a finite register from a toggle substrate. **The Standard Model is the dominant member of that class under the empirical labeling rule.** Specific identifications — which eigenvalue is which mass, which spinor is which fermion — are downstream of structure and validated empirically.

## The commitments are not all the same kind

- Self-containment is **metaphysical** — cannot be proved, only refused.
- Finite observer is **scoping** — it names the subject of the framework's predictions.
- Active reading is **interpretive** — adopted as the natural choice within the relational stance self-containment suggests.
- The empirical labeling rule is **empirical** — which math is which physics.

Conflating them produces apparent over-commitment. Naming them explicitly avoids the smuggle.

## The framework is structurally goal-free

No reference to particles, fields, or interactions enters until the empirical labeling rule attaches them. The structural chain holds whether or not any particular Standard Model observable matches measurement; the labeling is what makes contact with experiment, and validation is per-prediction. **Labeling-free statements** (the existence of complex Hilbert structure on the substrate, the local Clifford algebra at trivalent nodes, the rapid-decay continuum limit) are framework-internal theorems independent of any labeling choice.

## What the framework does NOT say

- It does not derive specific masses or mixing angles in advance of the empirical labeling rule; it derives the spectrum from which they are labeled.
- It does not derive Newton's constant $G$ or the absolute value of the cosmological constant $\Lambda$; those are calibrations against cosmological observation.
- It does not yet have a smooth-manifold continuum limit at journal grade; the general-relativistic sector rests on a partial closure.
- It does not tell you what makes a register an *observer* in any thicker sense than "finite-memory device" — the framework's scope is structural, not phenomenological.
