# The Story

A narrative arc from *what can exist?* to the Standard Model — the same chain the framework's rigorous documents walk, told as a story.

The chain is short. Three irreducible commitments, plus standard published mathematics, single out the srs crystal net — the dominant member of a small MDL-waterline survivor set — whose spectral content is the Standard Model. One empirical labeling rule attaches contact with experiment, and one measured scale calibrates the single dimensional unit. There are no further inputs.

We open with a question, name the commitments, watch the substrate emerge, see the cover that holds chirality and supplies the path to mass, trace how recurrence-filtering by a finite register forces complex Hilbert space, then walk to the Standard Model spectrum and the over-determination that distinguishes derivation from fitting.
