## Derivation

**Step 1.** ν_R is the SM-gauge-singlet fermion in the PS multiplet; its Majorana mass is generated by the substrate (no SM-gauge obstruction). It lives on the C_3-trivial Bloch direction at the P-point for *scale* (m_ν₃ closure), but the *generation* index runs over the three C_3 irreps {trivial, ω, ω²} (`predictions/R3_observer_c3_generation.py`). [Type 4 + ADOPTED-B3]

**Step 2.** B(P) (the Hashimoto walker operator at the P-point) commutes with the edge-lifted C_3, so it block-decomposes by C_3 irrep. **Sign-lock (Probe B, 2026-05-14):** on the Ramanujan subspace V_Ram ⊂ B(P), the ω-block carries the eigenvalue pair {+h, +h̄} (both with +Re part) and the ω²-block carries {−h, −h̄} (both with −Re part), where h = (√3+i√5)/2 and |h|² = k*−1 = 2. The trivial-C_3 sector of V_Ram is 4-dim and carries all four eigenvalues; outside V_Ram the trivial channel additionally contains the non-Ramanujan |h|=1 pair {+1,−1}. The Re-sign assignment ω ↔ +Re, ω² ↔ −Re is **theorem-grade** (Probe B verdict §5, an internal working note); a residual +Im-chirality convention picks h_ω = +h (over +h̄) and h_ω² = −h̄ (over −h) within each Re-sign-locked block. [Type 4 + Probe B; verified `proofs/foundations/probe_B_observer_SU3_on_V_Ram.py`, `proofs/foundations/majorana_M_R_waterfilling.py` step 1]

**Step 3 — ADOPTED-NU-MAJ-PHASE.** The ν_R Majorana mass on generation channel m is taken to carry one girth-ring's worth of walker holonomy:
$$M_R^{(m,m)} \;=\; |M_R|\cdot h_m^{\,g},\qquad |M_R| = \frac{\delta^4\,M_{\rm Pl}}{2\,k^*\,N_{\rm atoms}}.$$
The magnitude |M_R| is real and theorem-grade-conditional (m_ν₃ closure). The **phase factor h_m^g is an identification, not derived** — see §6. (Convention residual: of the two eigenvalues in each Re-sign-locked C_3 block — Step 2 — the *single complex* h_m is picked via +Im-chirality; the proper block trace is real. Post Probe B (2026-05-14) this is a residual ±Im pick, not the previous "+Im of one of four ±h, ±h̄".)

**Step 4 — seesaw.** With diagonal M_D = diag(m_u, m_c, m_t) in the PS up-quark mass eigenbasis [Type 3, PS structure under SU(4)] and the diagonal M_R from Step 3,
$$m_\nu \;=\; M_D\,M_R^{-1}\,M_D^{\!\top} \;=\; {\rm diag}\!\Big(\tfrac{m_{u_m}^2}{|M_R|}\,h_m^{-g}\Big),$$
complex symmetric (diagonal). [Type 3]

**Step 5 — Takagi + PMNS phase extraction.** The Takagi factorisation m_ν = U·D·U^⊤ with D real positive gives U = diag(e^{iφ_m/2}) with φ_m = −g·arg(h_m); in the standard PMNS parameterisation U_PMNS = V_Dirac·diag(1, e^{iα_21/2}, e^{iα_31/2}) with V_Dirac = I here, comparing phases (NuFIT ordering: light masses ordered, phases referenced to the lightest) yields
$$\alpha_{21} \;=\; g\cdot\arg(h_\omega) \;\bmod 360^\circ.$$
[Type 2]
