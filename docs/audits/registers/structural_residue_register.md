# Structural Residue Register

**Date opened:** 2026-04-26.
**Status:** Living document. Canonical register of all soft-gated structural alternatives in the framework.

## Purpose

Every row of `uniqueness_ledger.md` identifies an *operator-permitted alternative set* — the mathematical alternatives the framework's structural axioms allow at each layer — and a *selection criterion* that picks the framework's claim out of that set. Alternatives the criterion eliminates fall into two kinds:

- **Hard-gated.** Eliminated by an exact algebraic zero (Fisher rank zero, kernel of partial trace, parity selection rule, dimensional analysis, structural impossibility). These leave no downstream signature and are not registered here.
- **Soft-gated.** Eliminated by a finite margin — typically MDL bit-cost, energy suppression, or implicit upstream filter. Under A2-T waterline reading, *every above-waterline representation is retained simultaneously* (`../../framework/framework_axioms.md` §3); a soft-gated alternative therefore carries a non-zero MDL Boltzmann-style weight and may produce *downstream artifacts* the framework has not yet computed.

This register tracks every soft-gated alternative the structural audit has surfaced, as **R-N** entries with status flags, downstream observables, and concrete tests to close.

## Why this is load-bearing

The framework's existing dark-correction MDL synthesis is exactly this pattern at the *parameter* level: couplings retain every above-waterline functional with explicit MDL weights, and observables sum over them. R-12 (chirality both-hands retention → SU(2)_L parity violation, Im(h)/|h| dark term) is the framework's worked example of a soft-gated *structural* alternative that became load-bearing physics. The register's hypothesis: more structural residues are doing similar work, currently uncatalogued.

Several entries below are direct candidate explanations for things the framework currently treats as separate derivations or `derive`-mode placeholders (vacuum origin, +1 time dimension, SU(2)_L gauge structure). Closing them would unify currently-disjoint pieces of the framework.

## Status vocabulary

- **OPEN.** Suspicion only. Alternative identified, soft-gating margin known, downstream signature suspected but not computed.
- **TRACED.** Downstream signature identified and order-of-magnitude estimated. Magnitude small enough to be invisible to current data, or too large to be ruled out without specific experiment.
- **ACCOUNTED-FOR.** The residue is the framework's existing derivation of a confirmed observable. Catalogued as a worked example so the methodology is concrete.
- **REFUTED.** Downstream check has been done and shows no signature; the alternative is in fact hard-gated by a downstream filter the audit had not noticed.

## Discipline rule

Every new prediction file or theorem doc must check this register for residues affecting its target observable, and either include the residue's contribution or argue why it is hard-gated for that observable. Same pattern as `../../parameters/parameter_linter.md` Type 1/2/3/4 gate, applied to residues. New predictions that close, refute, or refine a residue must update its entry below.
