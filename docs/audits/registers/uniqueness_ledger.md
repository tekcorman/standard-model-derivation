# Uniqueness Ledger — bottom-up audit of structural claims

**Date opened:** 2026-04-26.
**Status:** Living document. Structural pass in progress.
**Purpose.** For every load-bearing structural claim in the framework, characterise the *operator-permitted alternative set* (what `../../operator_sweep/operator_sweep_from_A1.md` allows) and the *selection criterion* that picks the framework's claim out of that set. Surface every place the framework says "forced" when it actually means "selected", and every place where no selection criterion is on file.

**Scope (this pass).** Structural claims only — alphabet, group, lattice, dimension, field, algebra, gauge group, generation count, etc. Parameter claims (5/12, 9/40, 256/6305, …) are deferred to a second pass.

**Order.** Bottom-up from {A1, P1'} along the operator-sweep DAG (`../../operator_sweep/operator_sweep_from_A1.md`).

---

## Status vocabulary

The primary status classifies the *strength* of the selection within the operator-permitted alternative set:

- **UNIQUE.** Every operator-permitted alternative other than the claim is *strictly eliminated* by the selection criterion (e.g., produces a contradiction, fails a structural premise, or has zero compression benefit by an exact algebraic argument). The claim is the only mathematically possible answer.
- **DOMINANT.** The claim minimises the selection criterion (typically MDL) within a non-empty set of operator-permitted alternatives. Other alternatives exist and would be admissible if the criterion were relaxed, but the claim wins by a strictly positive margin. The margin is explicit and bounded below.
- **ONE-AMONG-MANY.** Multiple operator-permitted alternatives clear the selection criterion with no clear dominance — either the criterion is degenerate on the alternative set, or multiple alternatives meet it simultaneously. The framework retains the multiplicity (e.g., A2-T waterline) or arbitrarily picks one (which is itself a gap).

Two orthogonal flags qualify the primary status:

- **CONDITIONAL.** The classification holds *given* an upstream theorem or premise that is itself established by another row or by an external derivation. The conditional dependency is named explicitly (e.g., "CONDITIONAL on Row 3"). If the upstream falls, the row's status falls with it.
- **GAP.** The selection chain at this row's own layer has a missing step — either the alternative set is not enumerated, an alternative is not eliminated, or the criterion invokes an unjustified premise. The gap is named so it can be closed by future work. A row with a GAP cannot be classified as UNIQUE/DOMINANT/ONE-AMONG-MANY at its own layer; the primary status reads "GAP" and the would-be classification is recorded as a hypothesis ("would be DOMINANT if the gap closes as expected").

## Conventions

Each row has the columns:

- **Claim.**
- *Source.* Canonical doc/predictions file where the claim is stated.
- *Operations invoked.* The numbered operations from `../../operator_sweep/operator_sweep_from_A1.md` that the source derivation actually uses, plus any external theorems cited.
- *Alternatives.* The operator-permitted alternative set at the upstream-most layer of the operator sweep at which the claim is well-posed.
- *Selection.* The criterion that eliminates non-claim alternatives.
- *Status.* UNIQUE / DOMINANT / ONE-AMONG-MANY, plus CONDITIONAL/GAP flags.
- *Margin.* (DOMINANT only) The size of the gap to the next-best alternative, with units.
- *Conditional on.* Upstream rows or external premises.
- *Gap.* What is missing in the derivation, if anything.
- *Filtered-alternative residue.* For every soft-gated discarded alternative, the corresponding **R-N** entry in `structural_residue_register.md`. Hard-gated alternatives are listed as "(hard-gated, no residue)" with the gating mechanism named. This field is what makes the ledger a physical-discovery instrument rather than only an audit record: each soft-gated alternative is a candidate downstream signature the framework may not have computed.

Rows are numbered for cross-reference; numbers are stable once assigned.
