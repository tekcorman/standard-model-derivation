# Theorem (sketch): Observer-side time is the martingale index

**Date:** 2026-05-25
**Status:** SKETCH GRADE (P1.O3 of the path-to-dynamics plan). The statement is standard probability theory applied to the framework's specific setup; the *content* is the identification of N (observation count) as the framework's unique natural observer-side discrete time index.
**Probe:** `proofs/foundations/observer_martingale_walk_2026-05-25.py` — all three falsification gates PASS.

## Statement

Let:
- **(srs walk)** an actual non-backtracking walk on srs, with Jaynes-uniform transitions (each of the 2 NB continuations chosen with probability 1/2 per `walker_dynamics_derivation.md` §4a).
- **(observer)** a Bayesian agent observing the sequence of toggle events (`walker_dynamics_derivation.md` Steps 2–5) that compress to the NB walk.
- **(filtration)** F_N = σ-algebra generated by the first N observations; F_0 ⊂ F_1 ⊂ F_2 ⊂ ⋯ is the observer's information ascent.
- **(posterior process)** for any F_∞-measurable indicator A, the Bayesian posterior process M_N = P(A | F_N) is well-defined.

**Claim.** Under the framework's A2-T (`theorem_toggle_from_self_containment.md` Step 1: Jaynes-uniform on toggle events from self-containment + no-privilege), the posterior process M_N is a **bounded martingale** with respect to the filtration {F_N}. The natural index of this martingale is N (the count of observations), and **observer-side time** is defined as

$$
t_{\text{observer}} = N \cdot t_P
$$

where t_P is the framework's substrate-primitive time unit (one toggle event = one Planck tick under the standard identification, `predictions/e_bit.py` + Landauer chain). Doob's martingale convergence theorem then gives, under the true measure (the Jaynes-uniform law that the framework predicts), the *consistency* M_N → 1_A a.s. as N → ∞.

## Why N is the *unique* natural index

The framework's information-flow geometry forces a single time index on the observer's filtration:

1. **A2-T forces i.i.d. observation structure.** Per Jaynes 1957 + (A) no-privilege (`walker_dynamics_derivation.md` §4a), each NB continuation at a directed edge is uniform 1/2 over the 2 valid continuations. Each observation is independent given the past directed-edge state — a homogeneous Markov chain on directed edges.
2. **The filtration is monotone in observation count.** Every observation adds exactly one bit of new information (at the binary NB choice); F_N strictly refines F_{N-1} except in degenerate (probability-0) cases. So N counts the observer's σ-algebra refinement steps.
3. **Bayesian-posterior martingale property** (Doob 1953; Lévy upward theorem). For any F_∞-measurable A and any prior on the underlying parameter space, E[M_{N+1} | F_N] = M_N. This is an *algebraic identity*, not a probabilistic property requiring extra structure.
4. **No competing natural index.** Real-valued time (Planck-tick-continuous) is *not* the natural index — the observer's σ-algebra only refines at discrete observation events, and there is no observer-side fact about "what happens between observations" (cf. `framework_qft_ontology.md` on the substrate's a-temporal multiway structure). The cascade clock H = 1/(N·t_P) (`H_0.py` derivation) is a downstream consequence, not an independent input.

The combination gives: **t_observer = N · t_P** is the unique observer-side time, forced by A2-T applied to the Bayesian filtration.

## Proof sketch

Three facts, none new in isolation:

**(1) The framework's setup makes observations i.i.d. given the directed-edge state.** From `walker_dynamics_derivation.md` Step 4a (Jaynes-uniform NB transitions) + Step 5 (causal state = directed edge). The Markov property is exact in the framework's compressed observation language.

**(2) The Bayesian posterior is a martingale on the observer's filtration.** Standard probability: for any prior π on a parameter space Θ, any observation likelihood p(obs | θ), and any F_∞-measurable random variable A,

$$
P(A \mid F_N) = \int_\Theta P(A \mid \theta) \pi(\theta \mid F_N) \, d\theta
$$

defines a process satisfying E[P(A | F_{N+1}) | F_N] = P(A | F_N) by the tower property of conditional expectation (Williams 1991, *Probability with Martingales* §10.5). This is a *consequence* of the σ-algebra structure and the prior; no extra assumption needed.

**(3) The index N is the framework's natural choice because of (1).** Step (1) makes each observation a self-contained σ-algebra-refining event; the observer's clock is the count of such events. The identification t = N · t_P fixes the unit by relating to the substrate's primitive time (one toggle = t_P; `e_bit.py`).

## Probe verification (`proofs/foundations/observer_martingale_walk_2026-05-25.py`)

Setup: simulate 5000 NB walks of 200 steps under the true (Jaynes-uniform) measure. Observer holds two hypotheses with prior 1/2 each: H_uniform (truth) and H_biased (alternative with 0.7/0.3 NB-continuation weights). Track the posterior P_N(H_uniform) along each walk.

Gates verified:

- **(a)** Marginal-predictive martingale property — E_marg[P_{N+1} | F_N] = P_N exactly (algebraic identity, verified to machine precision across 12 sample realizations at N = 10, 50, 100, 150).
- **(b)** Doob L² convergence — under truth, mean P_N(H_uniform) → 0.998 by N = 200 with Var(N=200) / Var(N=10) = 0.019.
- **(c)** Convergence rate matches the theoretical KL divergence — measured log-odds slope 0.0876 vs theoretical KL(H_uniform ∥ H_biased) = 0.0872 (0.5% deviation).

## What this gives the framework

A clean, framework-internal statement of observer-side time:

- **Discrete time:** observation count N, unit t_P, identified t_observer = N · t_P.
- **Martingale property:** the observer's beliefs about F_∞-events are well-defined (Bayesian) processes that respect the σ-algebra refinement; no "non-Markov" jumps or non-causal updates.
- **Consistency (Doob):** under the framework's true measure, the observer's posterior converges to the truth — beliefs are calibrated by accumulating observations.
- **Companion of substrate-side P1.S4:** the substrate side has a discrete causal cone on B^L (P1.S4: max graph distance ≤ L bonds per step). The observer side has a martingale index N on F_N. Together: discrete-time on both sides, *but with different operational content*. Substrate-side L is a causal-cone counter; observer-side N is an information-refinement counter. They coincide structurally (both substrate primitives) but are operationally distinct.

## Limitations / what's *not* claimed

- This theorem doesn't say anything about a *unitary* propagator (substrate-side or observer-side). Quantum dynamics still requires either Lindblad evolution (P1.S3, built) or a Szegedy/Grover-style unitarization of the substrate walk (not built).
- The "discrete time on both sides" picture is two clocks, not one. The framework will need a story for how t_substrate (substrate primitive ticks, equivalent to L in B^L) and t_observer (observation count N) relate — they need not agree per substrate primitive event since not every substrate toggle produces an observation (cf. A2-T canonicalization erasing backtracks, `walker_dynamics_derivation.md` §4a parenthetical).
- The "unique natural index" claim relies on the framework's A2-T forcing i.i.d. observations. If A2-T is weakened, other indices (e.g., real-valued time with diffusion) become possible.
