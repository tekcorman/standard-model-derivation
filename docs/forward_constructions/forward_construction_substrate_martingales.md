## Result (preview)

**Two distinct martingale results emerge for the framework's substrate, both with information-theoretic-Noether interpretations:**

1. **Bayesian-posterior martingale on multi-admissible MDL.** A2-T's plural-retention form (Step 8: above-waterline models retained as a Bayesian mixture) is a martingale under the substrate observation filtration. Result: **the expectation of any model functional under the running posterior is conserved (martingale)**. Csiszár-Matuš 2003 §5; Williams 1991 *Probability with Martingales* §10.

2. **I-projection sub-martingale via data-processing.** When the model family 𝒬 contracts (coarser-grained substrate σ-algebras), KL divergence D(P ‖ I-projection_𝒢) is a sub-martingale. Result: **mutual information with the underlying substrate is non-increasing under coarse-graining** (data-processing inequality + I-projection geometry).

Together these give a **two-sided structural account of substrate conservation**:
- Under *time-translation* (filtration growth without coarse-graining): exact conservation (martingale).
- Under *coarse-graining* (filtration contraction): monotone information loss (sub-martingale).

This is the substrate's information-theoretic analog of:
- Noether's theorem (energy conservation under time-translation in QFT)
- H-theorem / second law (entropy increase under coarse-graining)

The framework grounds *both* in I-projection theory + standard probability martingale theory.

## 1. Setup — substrate observation filtration

By A1 + P1' + A2-T (`../theorems/theorem_A2_mdl_from_finite_register.md`), the substrate is F_inv(E)'s Cayley graph; the observer accumulates substrate observations over time. Define the **observation filtration** {𝒢_n}_{n ∈ ℕ}:

- **𝒢_0** = trivial σ-algebra (no observations yet).
- **𝒢_n** = σ-algebra generated by substrate observations through time step n.
- **𝒢_∞** = σ(∪_n 𝒢_n) = full observation σ-algebra at infinite time.

Filtration property: 𝒢_n ⊂ 𝒢_{n+1} (each observation refines the previous σ-algebra). This is the natural filtration of the toggle Markov chain at the substrate level (Layer 4.11).

For the multiway / non-backtracking-walk version, replace "time step" with "NB-walk extension by one step"; the filtration is then the natural NB-walk filtration on F_inv(E)'s Cayley graph.

## 2. Bayesian-posterior martingale (plural A2-T)

### 2.1 Setup

By A2-T Step 8 (`../theorems/theorem_A2_mdl_from_finite_register.md` §11), the multi-admissible regime of MDL retains all above-waterline models as a Bayesian mixture (Grünwald 2007 §17). Let:

- **𝒬** = model class.
- **π_0(Q)** = prior over models (uniform over above-waterline; weighted by compression savings).
- **π_n(Q) = π(Q | x_1, ..., x_n)** = posterior over models after observations x_1, ..., x_n.

The framework's "compressed view at time n" is the posterior-weighted mixture ∫ Q · π_n(Q) dQ.

### 2.2 Martingale property

**Theorem (Bayesian-posterior martingale; Williams 1991 *Probability with Martingales* §10.7).** Under the prior measure π_0, the posterior π_n(Q) is a martingale w.r.t. the observation filtration {𝒢_n}:

$$\mathbb{E}_{\pi_0}[\pi_{n+1}(Q) \mid \mathcal{G}_n] = \pi_n(Q)$$

This is the standard Bayesian-martingale theorem (Williams 1991 §10.7; Doob's martingale).

### 2.3 Substrate conservation finding

**Corollary.** For any bounded model functional f: 𝒬 → ℝ, the running posterior expectation E_{π_n}[f(Q)] is a martingale under π_0:

$$\mathbb{E}_{\pi_0}\big[\mathbb{E}_{\pi_{n+1}}[f(Q)] \mid \mathcal{G}_n\big] = \mathbb{E}_{\pi_n}[f(Q)]$$

**Substrate interpretation.** The framework's *posterior expectation of any model functional* (e.g., the Bayesian-mixture mass-content of a substrate observable) is conserved under the no-coarse-graining observation filtration. This is the substrate's analog of energy conservation: under time-translation invariance of the toggle process, the running posterior expectation of any model functional stays at its prior-conditional expectation.

### 2.4 Comparison to Noether

| Standard QFT | Substrate analog |
|---|---|
| Continuous time-translation invariance of action | Statistical time-translation invariance of toggle Markov chain (constant p_create = 1/2, p_destroy = 1/3) |
| Conserved current J^μ with ∂_μ J^μ = 0 | Bayesian posterior martingale π_n(Q) |
| Conserved charge Q = ∫ J^0 d³x | Conserved expectation E_{π_n}[f(Q)] |
| Energy conservation as exact theorem of EOM | Posterior martingale as exact theorem of conditional expectation |

The substrate's conservation is *information-theoretic*: what is conserved is the posterior expectation of any model functional, not a physical energy. Under the framework's identification of physical observables with model-functional expectations, this becomes a conservation law for the framework's predicted observables.

## 3. I-projection sub-martingale under coarse-graining

### 3.1 Setup

Now consider a contracting filtration: {ℋ_n} with ℋ_n ⊃ ℋ_{n+1} (coarser σ-algebras). Each ℋ_n carries a model family 𝒬_n; let Q_n* = I-projection of source P onto 𝒬_n.

By the A2-T-as-I-projection result (`forward_construction_a2t_as_iprojection.md` §5.3), under nested exponential families, sequential I-projection through {𝒬_n} factors through the hierarchy.

### 3.2 Sub-martingale property

**Theorem (I-divergence sub-martingale; Csiszár-Matuš 2003 Corollary 4 / data-processing).** For nested I-projection sequence {Q_n*} as above, the D-divergences satisfy:

$$D(P \,\|\, Q_n^*) \leq D(P \,\|\, Q_{n+1}^*)$$

i.e., the irreducible compression cost is non-decreasing as the model family contracts.

Equivalently (via Csiszár-Pythagorean):

$$D(P \,\|\, Q_n^*) = D(P \,\|\, Q_{n+1}^*) - D(Q_n^* \,\|\, Q_{n+1}^*)$$

(under exponential-family conditions; otherwise this is an inequality).

### 3.3 Substrate interpretation

**Coarse-graining loses information (monotonically).** As the framework's compression hierarchy contracts the model family (e.g., from full Bloch decomposition to a coarser per-Brillouin-point decomposition), the irreducible compression cost rises. This is structurally identical to the H-theorem in statistical mechanics: coarse-graining produces monotone information loss / entropy increase.

**Comparison to second law of thermodynamics.**

| Standard | Substrate analog |
|---|---|
| H-theorem: Boltzmann's H is non-increasing under collision dynamics | I-divergence D(P‖Q_n*) is non-decreasing under coarse-graining |
| Entropy increase under coarse-graining | Compression cost increase under coarse-graining |
| Arrow of time from coarse-graining | Arrow of compression hierarchy from coarse-graining |

The framework grounds the H-theorem direction in I-projection theory: the *direction* of irreversibility in the framework's compression hierarchy is the information-loss direction.

## 4. Two-sided substrate conservation account

Combining Sections 2 and 3:

| Filtration direction | Behavior | Information-theoretic content |
|---|---|---|
| **Growing** {𝒢_n} (no coarse-graining; more observations) | Bayesian posterior martingale | Exact conservation of model-functional expectations |
| **Contracting** {ℋ_n} (coarse-graining; less resolution) | I-divergence sub-martingale | Monotone information loss |

The substrate's conservation structure is *two-sided*:
- **Forward** (more observations, no compression): conservation
- **Backward** (more compression, fewer details): monotone loss

Standard QFT separates these: time-translation gives Noether conservation; coarse-graining + entropy gives the second law. The substrate gives both within the same I-projection apparatus, applied to opposite filtration directions.

This is structurally satisfying: in standard physics, "conservation" and "second law" feel like distinct principles. In the substrate's information-theoretic foundation, they are corollaries of the *same* mathematical apparatus (martingale theory of I-projections).

## 6. Honest scope

1. **Bayesian-posterior martingale assumes prior π_0.** The result requires a specific prior over the model class. The framework's plural-retention rule (above-waterline models, weighted by compression savings) is a specific choice; the martingale theorem holds under this choice. Other priors give different martingales.

2. **I-divergence sub-martingale requires nested exponential families.** Section 3.2's strict equality (Csiszár-Pythagorean) requires this; otherwise the result is an inequality. The framework's compression hierarchies need explicit checking.

3. **No specific QFT-prediction emerges.** Like the A2-T-as-I-projection result, this is a *structural* finding (category-2 yield). It provides mathematical apparatus and conceptual grounding, not new numerical SM-predictions.

4. **The information-theoretic Noether is information-theoretic, not energy-theoretic.** The substrate's conserved quantity is the running posterior expectation of model functionals, not a physical energy. Identifying these with physical conserved quantities requires a separate identification step (Layer 6 cosmology / energy-momentum tensor work).

5. **Csiszár-Matuš martingale convergence.** The full strength of the martingale apparatus includes convergence theorems (e.g., posterior consistency). Section 2 used the martingale property; convergence follows from Williams 1991 §11 + standard Bayesian consistency theorems. Not exhaustively rederived here.
